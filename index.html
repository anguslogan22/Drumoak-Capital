Cohort PLC · The thesis in three lines

Why the discount should close

Key debate

A 28% multiple gap

Cohort trades on 12.9x LTM EV/EBITDA against a peer median of 17.9x. The market attributes the gap to size and liquidity relative to BAE Systems and L3Harris, not to a weaker business.

Drumoak view

No structural quality gap

Margins sit inside the peer range and revenue is underwritten by contracted NATO and UK rearmament spend. Adjusted EBIT margin moves from 11.8% to 15.0% by FY34E as mix shifts toward electronic warfare and sonar.

What would change it

Gross margin below 36%

FY27E to FY29E gross margin delivery is the key near-term data point. A stall below the 36% to 38% range, or slippage in UK procurement timelines, would challenge the view.

Process

How each note is built

Full methodology →
01

Frame the debate

Identify the single question the market is pricing, take a position on it, and organise every section of the note around it.

02

Model from the filings

Three-statement build from reported numbers, then a DCF cross-checked against trading comps, with residual income or DDM where the business warrants it.

03

Define what breaks it

Every note sets measurable signals that would strengthen or challenge the thesis, and the model is revisited when they appear.
