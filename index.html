Key Debate

Renew is commonly treated as a cyclical UK construction and engineering business, with its single-digit operating margin and AIM listing reinforcing the perception that it deserves a contractor-style valuation.

Drumoak View

Renew is better understood as an asset-light critical infrastructure services compounder. Regulatory spending visibility, cost-plus framework contracts, strong cash conversion and high returns on capital support a structurally higher valuation than traditional UK contractors.
