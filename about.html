About

Angus Logan

Founder of Drumoak Capital. BCom Finance, Statistics minor, McGill University Desautels Faculty of Management.

Drumoak Capital is an independent research project I started in 2025 to build, and show, a repeatable investment process: frame the debate, model it from the filings, take a view and say what would prove it wrong.

The notes are written to sell-side standards, with a full DCF and trading comps as standard and residual income or DDM where the business calls for it. Coverage has narrowed over time towards defence, infrastructure, space and TMT, with credit and restructuring work alongside the equity calls.

I am currently an off-cycle Investment Analyst Intern at Galliant Advisors LP, an equity long/short fund in Montreal, and will join Bank of America in London as an Investment Banking Summer Analyst in EMEA Equity Capital Markets in 2027. Before that I worked in PwC's Digital Audit team in London and in Raymond James Private Client Wealth Management in Montreal.

I grew up between Scotland, Singapore and Canada. Outside research I am Vice-Captain of the McGill Redbirds varsity soccer team and Co-Founder and Co-President of McGill Athletes in Business.

Methodology

How I build a note

01

Frame the central debate

Before the financials, I identify the single question the market is debating. It becomes the organising principle of the note, and every section connects back to it.

02

Understand the business

Competitive position, unit economics, distribution and management track record. The aim is a clear view of what makes the business durable or fragile before looking at reported numbers.

03

Anchor on the right metrics

Separate the KPIs that reflect underlying demand from accounting and timing noise, and build the forecast around those drivers.

04

Triangulate the valuation

A DCF with explicit WACC build and sensitivity, cross-checked against trading comps, and residual income or DDM where relevant. Weighting caps the influence of the most assumption-sensitive method.

05

Set the monitoring framework

Specific, measurable signals that would strengthen or challenge the thesis. This is what separates a live process from a one-off write-up.

Be honest about uncertainty, disciplined about evidence, and improve with every report.
